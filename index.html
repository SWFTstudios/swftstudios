(FAQ)

Common questions

What does SWFT stand for?

Strategic Workflows Facilitating Transformation. In plain words: we get you seen, get you chosen and get you booked, then automate what works so it keeps happening.

Who do you work with?

Service businesses, e-commerce brands, restaurants and hospitality, B2B and professional services, creators and personal brands, and health, wellness and beauty companies, mostly in Jersey City, New York City and North Jersey.

How much does a website cost?

Websites start at $400 for up to 5 pages with base features when you supply photos and copy. Website plus on-site photo and video starts at $2,000. Larger and multi-location projects start at $3,000. Final prices depend on agreed scope. See pricing for every service.

How fast can you turn around a site?

Simpler builds can turn around in as little as 24 to 48 hours when content and decisions are ready. Larger sites, multi-stakeholder projects and shoot-inclusive work take longer. We set a realistic timeline after intake.

Do I need to book a call first?

No. You can send project details or start with the free Growth Audit. If you’d rather talk, book a 30-minute call straight from our calendar.

Do you require long-term contracts?

No. Project work is scoped and billed per engagement. Retainers are month-to-month.
